Bornomala


----------------------------------------------------------------
PROBLEM YOU PLAN TO TACKLE  (138 words / 150 limit)
----------------------------------------------------------------

In July 2024, the state cut 3G and 4G. Broadband went dark. What
survived was 2G — voice and SMS. People fell back to text.

But Bangla text is not in the GSM-7 alphabet, so every Bangla SMS
is forced into UCS-2 encoding: 70 characters per segment instead
of English's 160. Bangla costs 2.3x more to send, splits into more
segments, and fails more often on a congested network — exactly
when SMS is the only channel left.

Bornomala is an offline compression codec for Bangla built for the
SMS segment boundary. A small statistical language model drives an
integer arithmetic coder, packing the output into GSM-7. Target:
roughly 200 Bangla characters inside a single 160-byte segment.

It ships as an installable offline app: compose, compress, send by
SMS, decode on arrival. No server, no internet, works on low-end
phones.

----------------------------------------------------------------
PLANNED TECH STACK
----------------------------------------------------------------

Offline-first PWA (vanilla JS + service worker, no framework,
small footprint).

Codec: small Bangla context model trained offline, exported as
integer probability tables; deterministic integer arithmetic coder
in WASM/JS; GSM-7 septet packing.

SMS path with no server: sms: URI to prefill the device SMS app
for sending; Web Share Target API to receive shared SMS text back
into the PWA for decoding; paste fallback.

Static hosting on GitHub Pages. Benchmark harness vs gzip and raw
UCS-2.

Stretch: Capacitor Android build.

KEY FACTS BEHIND THE PITCH  (verified)
================================================================

- Grameenphone / Telenor records: 3G and 4G were shut down during
  the July 2024 blackout; 2G stayed up — voice and SMS. SMS was
  not a fallback theory, it was the channel that survived.

- GSM 03.38: 7-bit alphabet gives 160 chars per SMS segment.
  Bangla is not in GSM-7 basic or extension tables, so it is
  forced to UCS-2 — 70 chars per segment. A 2.3x penalty.

- Shutdown ran 18-28 July 2024 (10 days), phased: throttle,
  mobile data cut, broadband cut, broadband back 24 July, mobile
  back 28 July, social platforms blocked until 31 July. Second
  blackout 4-5 August. VPNs (Proton, Nord, TunnelBear) blocked.

FRAMING TO LEAD WITH  (posts, README, demo video)
================================================================

July 2024. The state cuts 3G and 4G. Broadband goes dark.
Facebook, YouTube, TikTok — gone. What survives is 2G: voice
and SMS.

So people fall back to text. And Bangla text gets 70 characters
per SMS. English gets 160.

Because Bangla is not in the GSM-7 alphabet, every Bangla message
is forced into UCS-2 — 2.3x the cost, 2.3x the segments, 2.3x the
failures on a congested network. In the days when SMS was the only
thing left standing, the language people were dying for was the
expensive one to send.

Bangladesh has fought this fight before. 1952 was about the right
to speak Bangla. 2024 was about the right to speak at all. This is
the same fight, one layer down — in the character encoding.
